Victoria L. Franklin is a doctoral researcher and data scientist specializing in geospatial analytics, artificial intelligence, environmental health, and critical infrastructure resilience. Her interdisciplinary research includes three peer-reviewed conference papers accepted for publication, with contributions spanning energy justice, cybersecurity, infrastructure resilience and community preparedness, and wastewater epidemiology.

**Research expertise:** AI & Computational Data Science · Geospatial Science & Environmental Justice · Public Health & Wastewater Epidemiology · Critical Infrastructure & Cyber-Physical Resilience
