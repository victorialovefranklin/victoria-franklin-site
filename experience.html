Research approach

The questions that guide my research.

My research asks how data, AI, and geospatial intelligence can make critical systems safer, healthier, and more equitable, and how to keep those tools explainable to the people who rely on them.

Across nuclear cybersecurity, wastewater genomic surveillance, and energy resilience, I build methods that are transparent, reproducible, and grounded in community impact: explainable AI for operational technology, geospatial analysis of who is covered and who is missed, and decision-support tools that leaders and the public can actually use.

01How can AI detect threats to critical infrastructure, and explain why?
02Which communities does public-health surveillance reach, and which does it miss?
03What can wastewater, genomic, and geospatial data reveal before a crisis is visible?
04How do power outages and climate hazards fall unevenly across communities?
05How can research become tools that leaders and the public can actually use?
I believe there is strength in knowing who you are while remaining graceful enough to continue learning.

Research & publications

Work at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, geospatial analytics, and public health.

My research brings together artificial intelligence, cyberinfrastructure, digital twins, geospatial intelligence, genomics, and environmental health analytics to strengthen public health preparedness, critical infrastructure resilience, and national security.

Research area 01

AI, Digital Twins & Nuclear Cybersecurity

AI-enabled digital-twin ecosystems for operational technology security, cyber-physical infrastructure protection, cyber risk assessment, and resilience of critical infrastructure.

Research area 02

Genomic Epidemiology & Biosurveillance

Integrating wastewater, environmental, genomic, and public health data for infectious-disease surveillance and early warning, backed by wet-lab and dry-lab metagenomics training.

Research area 03

Climate Resilience, Environmental Justice & Energy Systems

How energy systems, infrastructure vulnerability, environmental burden, and social determinants of health connect, including outage-burden modeling and resilience planning.

Research area 04

Geospatial Public Health & Ecosystem Analytics

Spatial epidemiology, built-environment and health-disparities research, urban forestry, carbon sequestration, and ecosystem valuation with GIS, remote sensing, and Google Earth Engine.

Dissertation research · In development

Nuclear Sentinel AI

An explainable, AI-enabled digital-twin framework for dynamic cyber-physical risk assessment and resilience in nuclear operational technology. A related manuscript is planned for the International Conference on Mathematics and Computational Methods Applied to Nuclear Science and Engineering (M&C 2027).

Alongside it, I am developing BioGrid Sentinel AI, which links cyberinfrastructure, environmental monitoring, genomics, and public health intelligence to support radiation surveillance and emergency preparedness in communities near nuclear facilities.
