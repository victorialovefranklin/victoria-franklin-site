My approach

Technology that serves people

Effective technology should do more than process information. It should help people understand complex problems, make better-informed decisions, strengthen systems, and deliver meaningful results.

Across government, scientific research, and technology development, I bring analytical rigor, responsible innovation, collaboration, and a commitment to public value. My goal is to build solutions that connect technical capability with real-world needs.

I believe there is strength in knowing who you are while remaining graceful enough to continue learning.

The questions that guide my work:

  1. 01Why do systems work the way they do?
  2. 02Who benefits from them, and who is overlooked?
  3. 03What can data reveal that we cannot see on the surface?
  4. 04How can technology solve problems without losing sight of the people it is meant to serve?
  5. 05And most importantly, what can I contribute?
